How it works

Five steps from "I should upskill" to a plan you can stick to

Onboarding

Job title, industry and daily tasks. Choose a goal (keep your job, get promoted or switch careers), do a quick skills check and a short brain test of how you study.

Skill selection

AI proposes 3–5 ranked skills. Each shows why it matters, the time to reach a useful level and the typical cost.

Time & package

Sync your calendars (private events stay hidden). We show the hours you realistically have, with a buffer and a burnout check.

Plan building

Pick from 2–3 courses designed by our specialists to fit your hours, all included in your package. They go straight into your timetable.

Track & replan

Track progress and see the payoff. Miss a session and AI replans it. Stuck? Book a specialist session.

All courses are designed by our in-house team of specialists. Linking to outside courses would mean users enrol themselves and follow someone else's fixed plan, which is exactly the effort we remove.

Expansion plan · B2B2C

Next: training whole workforces for businesses that must reinvent themselves

Governments are pushing for greener production, so many companies must retrain employees to make that transition. 52% of CEOs say a lack of skills in their workforce makes it harder to reinvent their business model (PwC, 2024).

  • Discovery meeting with the business: headcount, skills needed, course length, time per day/week
  • The same individual journey for each employee: brain test, calendar, progress tracking, replanning
  • A skills library built by mentors + AI makes every new course faster to design (updated regularly)
  • Covers all fields, including manual labour

Why later, not now?

B2B2C needs a much larger and more varied team of experts and a stronger AI model. That takes capital, so we'll first build revenue from individual subscriptions and specialist sessions to fund it. Business pricing will be set separately.
